How to read this

Calendar lists one-off things with a date — meetings, tryouts, games, deadlines, days off. It opens on today and runs forward; switch to Past to look back. The three weekly standing meetings sit in the box at the top instead, since they'd otherwise fill the calendar with repeats.

Clubs & teams is the standing picture of each group: where it meets, who sponsors it, what it costs. Heard is the last date it came up on air.

Everything here is read from the auto-generated captions on the school's own announcement videos, so treat rooms, times and spellings as good-but-not-certain — the captions mangle names badly, and anything two videos disagreed about is flagged rather than quietly resolved. This is a student-made summary, not an official school publication; check with the sponsor before you count on a time.
